🔍 Active Driver Audit

Finds any car with more than one "active" driver record (no end date). These are the records inflating your active count.

Click Run Audit to scan your driver database.
🏢 Fleet Mismatches

The CARS tab decides which entity owns a car — fines are always nominated under that entity. This finds drivers whose stored fleet disagrees with it (e.g. the driver says TBM but the car is XE BEC), which is exactly what would put a fine under the wrong company.

Click Run Check to scan for mismatches.
⚖️ Driver / Car Reconciliation

Explains the gap between your active driver count and active car count. Shows exactly where the numbers diverge — duplicates, drivers on cars not yet in the fleet, drivers on sold/inactive cars, and active cars with nobody on them.

Click Run Reconciliation to break down the gap.
🛠️ Sheet Maintenance

Rearrange the CARS sheet columns into the new logical order (engine number next to VIN, fuel next to shape, odometer/service/docs grouped together) and write a header row. Run this once after updating the app.

⚠ Back up first. Open your Google Sheet, right-click the CARS tab → Duplicate. Then run the migration. It reads every row, rearranges it, and writes it back. Do this before adding or editing any cars in the new version.
🩺 Column Alignment Check

Checks each CARS row individually to see whether it's in the old or new column order. Symptoms of an old row: the Year box is blank and Make shows the model (e.g. Make = "CAMRY"). Unlike the full migration above, this only rewrites the rows that are actually misaligned — so it's safe on a partly-migrated sheet where some rows are already correct.

Click Scan to check every row.
📋 Default Rego Term

Sets any car with no rego term to 6 months. Cars that already have a term (3 / 6 / 12) are left exactly as they are — this only fills blanks.

Click Check to see how many cars are missing a rego term.
📅 Bulk Rent Due-Day Update

Sets the rent due day from your master rent sheet for every active driver, matched by rego. Where the sheet listed several days (e.g. WED-FRI) the last one is used. Rent amounts are not touched — only the day. Drivers marked red on your sheet are skipped. Nothing is written until you press Apply.

Click Preview to see what would change.
🧪 Connection Test

Checks the Cloudflare Worker and every sheet tab the app needs, and shows exactly what came back. Run this when data won’t load — it turns “failed to load” into a specific cause.

Click Run test.
⬇ Import Driver Data from QuickBooks

This tool pulls all invoices from QuickBooks, extracts rego + start date + rent for each driver, then cross-references with your payments sheet and drivers details sheet to build complete records. You review all matches before anything is written to the database.
